# Birth Certificate Registration — Required Documents Checklist

Service: Birth Certificate
queueId: q_birth

## Required documents
- Hospital record of birth (weighing card or delivery notification) — original.
- Ghana Card of the mother — original, not expired.
- Ghana Card of the father — original, not expired (required for joint registration).
- Completed birth registration form.

## Common mistakes that cause rejection
- Bringing a photocopy of the hospital record instead of the original.
- Only one parent's Ghana Card when both are required for a joint registration.
- Registering a birth older than 12 months without a statutory declaration (late registration requires extra documents).
- Mismatched spelling of the child's or parents' names across documents.

## Validity rules
- Both parents' Ghana Cards must be valid (not expired) on the appointment day.
- Late registration (child older than 12 months) requires a sworn statutory declaration in addition to the documents above.
- For a single parent, a statutory declaration of sole guardianship is required in place of the second parent's Ghana Card.
