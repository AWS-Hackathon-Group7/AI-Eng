# Passport Renewal — Required Documents Checklist

Service: Passport Renewal
queueId: q_passport

## Required documents
- Old passport (the one being renewed) — must be the original, not a photocopy.
- Ghana Card (National Identification Card) — original, not expired.
- One recent passport-size photograph — white background, taken within the last 6 months.
- Completed passport renewal application form.

## Common mistakes that cause rejection
- Bringing a photocopy of the old passport instead of the original.
- Ghana Card has expired or the name does not match the old passport.
- Photograph has a coloured background, or the applicant is wearing glasses/headwear.
- Old passport is damaged (torn pages, water damage) — a damaged passport needs a separate replacement process, not a renewal.

## Validity rules
- The Ghana Card must be valid (not expired) on the day of the appointment.
- Names across the old passport, Ghana Card, and application form must match exactly. A change of name requires supporting legal documents (e.g. marriage certificate, deed poll).
- Renewal is only available if the old passport expires within 6 months or has already expired.
