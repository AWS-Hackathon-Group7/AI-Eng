# Driver's License Renewal — Required Documents Checklist

Service: Driver's License Renewal
queueId: q_license

## Required documents
- Expiring or expired driver's license — the original card.
- Ghana Card (National Identification Card) — original, not expired.
- Eye test certificate from an approved optician — issued within the last 3 months.
- Completed license renewal application form.

## Common mistakes that cause rejection
- Eye test certificate older than 3 months, or from a non-approved optician.
- Bringing a photocopy of the old license instead of the original card.
- Ghana Card name does not match the driver's license name.
- Attempting to renew a license that has been suspended or has outstanding penalties.

## Validity rules
- The eye test certificate must be dated within 3 months of the appointment.
- The Ghana Card must be valid (not expired) on the appointment day.
- A license expired for more than 1 year may require a fresh theory/practical test instead of a simple renewal.
